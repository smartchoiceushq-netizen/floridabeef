How a beef share order works, according to the store

  1. Choose a quarter, half or whole cow based on your freezer space and family size.
  2. Pay the deposit online to reserve your share.
  3. The store schedules, butchers and vacuum-seals your beef. Shares take 2 to 5 weeks to prepare, and the store contacts you by text and email.
  4. You receive a final invoice based on the package weight, with the balance due.
  5. The order ships frozen, packed with dry ice, or you can arrange farm pickup with the store.

Good to know before you pay

  • Share deposits are non-refundable, according to the store. Read the refund policy before you pay.
  • The store ships to the lower 48 states. It lists free shipping on Eighth Beef Boxes and on orders over $250 by ground network.
  • Shipping protection is optional and handled by a third party. The store says it is not responsible for carrier loss, theft, delay or spoilage unless protection is bought at checkout.
  • On the shares product page the store shows about 4.5 out of 5 from 61 reviews. Most are positive. A few customers mention the fat content of ground beef and packaging problems, so read them on the store before you decide.
