Grass-fed and grass-finished beef, delivered to your door

The Eighth Beef Box

An eighth of a cow, cut and packed for your freezer. The store describes it as a way to sample a wide variety of cuts without buying a whole share. Details below come from the store's own page, checked on November 23, 2026.

  • At least 39 lb
  • About 104 six-ounce servings
  • Free shipping, as listed by the store

What is inside

  • 2 boneless ribeye steaks (16 oz average)
  • 2 New York strip steaks (14 oz average)
  • 2 filet mignon steaks (8 oz average)
  • 2 sirloin baseball steaks (8 oz average)
  • 1 flank steak (8 oz average)
  • 1 flat iron (8 oz average)
  • 1 chuck eye steak (14 oz average)
  • 1 inside skirt (10 oz average)
  • 1 boneless chuck roast (3 lb average)
  • 1 London broil (2.25 lb average)
  • 1 rump roast (3.25 lb average)
  • 3 packs of stew meat (1 lb each)
  • 20 packs of 80/20 ground beef (1 lb each)

Good to know before you pay

  • Deposits are non-refundable, according to the store. Read its refund policy before you pay.
  • The store ships to the lower 48 states. It lists free shipping on orders over $250 by ground network, and two-day shipping from Florida to 20 states in the South, Midwest and Northeast.
  • Shipping protection is optional and handled by a third party. The store says it is not responsible for orders lost, stolen, damaged, delayed or spoiled in transit unless protection is bought at checkout. No cancellations or address changes are possible once an order is being prepared.
  • On the shares page the store shows about 4.5 out of 5 from 61 reviews. Most are positive. A few customers mention the fat content of ground beef and packaging problems, so read them on the store before you decide.
